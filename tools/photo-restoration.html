Start with a clear source

  • Works best on low-resolution photos, mild blur, image noise and JPEG compression.
  • First use downloads a compact AI model; processing speed depends on your phone or computer.
  • Output is 4× the original width and height. Very large images are limited to 1 megapixel for browser memory safety.
What this beta can and cannot do

This tool uses the general-purpose Real-ESRGAN x4v3 model for AI denoising and super-resolution. It may reconstruct plausible details rather than recover the exact original. It does not specifically repair scratches, tears, missing corners or faded colours, and results can look different from the source. Do not use AI-restored results as evidence or for identity verification. The image is processed locally in your browser; the model and runtime are downloaded from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. No photo is sent to a Zhatpat application server.
