Use small, precise marks

  • Paint over the entire object, including a small margin around its edges.
  • Works best for small distractions, dust, blemishes and objects on simple backgrounds.
  • Large objects, faces, text, patterns and complex backgrounds may leave visible artefacts.
How this tool works

This beta uses OpenCV's classical Telea inpainting algorithm, not a generative AI model. It estimates marked pixels from nearby image information; it cannot understand a scene or recreate hidden details reliably. The image is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a Zhatpat application server. OpenCV.js is loaded from the official OpenCV documentation CDN on first use.

Algorithm reference: OpenCV Inpainting documentation.
