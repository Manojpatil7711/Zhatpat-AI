A few simple tips

  • Use a clear, well-lit photo with the subject fully visible.
  • BiRefNet works with people and many everyday objects; very fine hair, transparent items and complex edges can still need manual touch-up.
  • The 512 × 512 model mask is resized to your image; tiny details, fine hair, transparent objects and motion blur may affect edge quality.
  • For memory safety, inputs above 24 megapixels are rejected; larger accepted images are downscaled to a maximum of about 6 megapixels for processing.
Privacy & model details

Your selected image is processed in this browser. BiRefNet Lite (about 94 MB, fp16) is downloaded from Hugging Face on first use and may be cached by your browser. Compatible devices use WebGPU for general foreground cutouts. If WebGPU is unavailable or cannot start the model, the tool tries a smaller general-purpose ISNet model on the CPU, then uses MODNet as a final portrait-only fallback. No Zhatpat application server receives your image.

Primary model: BiRefNet Lite 512 · MIT. CPU compatibility model: ISNet General INT8 · MIT. Final portrait fallback: MODNet · Apache 2.0.
